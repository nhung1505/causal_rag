% VII. DISCUSSION AND LIMITATIONS

\section{Discussion and Limitations}
\label{sec:discussion_limitations}

Full LegalSCM achieves 96.80\% decision accuracy, compared with 88.80\% for
Causal Path RAG. Because both systems share identical retrieval outputs and all
23 Full-only gains occur after verifier commitment, the improvement is
localized to committed factual validation and grounded direct-edge checking.
However, Answer F1, ROUGE-L, and Citation F1 do not improve significantly,
showing that structured decision correctness and free-form answer quality
remain distinct objectives.

The closed-book results should be interpreted cautiously. Their low accuracy
reflects the verification-oriented label contract, absence of retrieved legal
evidence, and strong class imbalance rather than general legal competence of
Qwen3-8B. This motivates reporting Balanced Accuracy and Macro-F1 alongside raw
Accuracy.

The experiment executes $do(X_m=0)$ on seven mediator-removal questions, but
Causal Path RAG also answers all seven correctly. The current results therefore
do not isolate a benefit from structural intervention. Likewise, although the
engine supports conjunctive rule bodies and alternative disjunctive mechanisms,
the imported rules contain only one positive antecedent per record. Both
intervention reasoning and multi-antecedent inference require larger dedicated
benchmarks.

Retrieval also remains a bottleneck. Top-1 Exact Path is 38.56\%, compared with
75.42\% Oracle Exact Path, and all eight Full errors occur after a
missing-primary-path fallback. Furthermore, the benchmark is small,
graph-derived, and constructed from the same structured resource used by the
system. Expert-reviewed and out-of-graph legal questions are needed to evaluate
external validity.

Finally, LegalSCM is a deterministic normative rule model, not a method for
causal discovery or statistical effect estimation. Its intervention semantics
are relative to the encoded legal mechanisms and do not include the exogenous
abduction required for unit-level actual-causation analysis.

% =========================================================
% VIII. CONCLUSION
% =========================================================
\section{Conclusion}
\label{sec:conclusion}

LegalSCM-RAG combines causal-path retrieval, three-valued rule inference, and
selective structural verification for Vietnamese multi-hop legal QA. On 250
questions, it improves decision accuracy over Causal Path RAG by 8.0 points
with paired statistical support, while answer and citation improvements are
not significant.

The results support selective factual and direct-claim verification in this
controlled setting, but do not yet establish the independent benefit of
$do(X_m=0)$. Future work will expand mediator-intervention and
multi-antecedent benchmarks, improve path ranking and grounding, enforce
answer--decision consistency, and conduct expert evaluation on legal questions
not derived from the source graph.
