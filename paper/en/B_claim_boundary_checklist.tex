\section{Claim boundary checklist}
\label{app:claims}

To prevent claim drift among the code, artifacts, and manuscript, the final
version must preserve the following boundaries:

\begin{enumerate}
    \item The graph is constructed from normalized legal rules; it is not
    discovered from observational data and does not provide causal-effect
    estimates.

    \item LegalSCM is a deterministic normative inference model over encoded
    events and rules; it is not a complete model of real-world causality or
    of the legal system as a whole.

    \item \texttt{path\_ablation} is a bounded node-deletion reachability
    baseline, not the $\doop(\cdot)$ operator under SCM semantics.

    \item Structural SCM and path ablation achieve identical quality metrics
    on the paired v4.1/v5.1 benchmark. There is no empirical evidence that
    the structural mode has higher accuracy.

    \item The observed benefit of the structural mode is its intervention
    semantics and more detailed traces; this benefit has not been evaluated
    through a user study or expert audit.

    \item Counterfactual Accuracy of $100\%$ reflects only the $20$
    direct-edge negative samples and does not fully validate hard-intervention
    validity.

    \item Top-1 Exact Path of $35.20\%$ is the deployable metric. Oracle
    Exact Path of $71.20\%$ is only an upper-bound diagnostic and must not be
    substituted for Top-1 in performance claims.

    \item Exact linear-path matching is not fully appropriate for branch and
    convergence structures; the $0\%$ result for these two groups must not be
    interpreted independently of the answer metrics and gold structure.

    \item The benchmark was generated from the same graph and has not been
    fully validated by independent legal experts. The results measure the
    recovery of an encoding within the scope of the study and do not
    demonstrate external legal correctness.

    \item Runtime is compared only within the same server environment used
    for the runs. Before absolute values are published, the hardware,
    software, and cache conditions must be reported.

    \item The system is a research prototype, not legal advice, and must not
    be used to make automated legal decisions about individuals.
\end{enumerate}

Every summary, slide, or translation of the paper must preserve these limits
and must not turn a diagnostic result into a stronger conclusion.
