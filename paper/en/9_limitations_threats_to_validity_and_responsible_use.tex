\section{Limitations, threats to validity, and responsible use}
\label{sec:limitations}

Although the experiments were designed as a paired comparison and all
$250$ samples were processed successfully, the results must be interpreted
within the scope of the current corpus, benchmark, and implementation. This
section analyzes threats to construct, internal, external, and conclusion
validity, while also defining the limits on the system's use.

\subsection{Construct validity}

\paragraph{Normative condition--effect relations are not equivalent to
experimentally established causal relations.}

The graph was constructed by operationalizing legal rules as normative
condition--effect relations. The \texttt{CAUSES} edges represent encoded
normative mechanisms, not causal relations discovered from observational
data or estimated through experiments. LegalSCM is therefore only a
structural causal model of legal norms within the scope of the given rule
representation. The result of an intervention $\doop(M=m)$ is the result of
a simulation on this model, not an estimate of a causal effect in the real
world \cite{pearl2009causality}.

\paragraph{The counterfactual subset does not fully measure hard
intervention.}

The twenty samples flagged with
\texttt{requires\_counterfactual=true} are primarily direct-edge negatives:
the graph contains a path $A\rightarrow M\rightarrow Y$ but does not contain
a direct edge $A\rightarrow Y$. They measure the ability to distinguish
direct from indirect relations more than the ability to predict a
counterfactual world following an intervention. Consequently, a
Counterfactual Accuracy of $100\%$ does not validate the general correctness
of structural intervention.

\paragraph{Linear gold paths do not cover nonlinear structures.}

Of the $250$ samples, only $236$ have linear gold paths. Four branch samples
and ten convergence samples do not have a single chain that fully represents
the structure required to answer the question, yet the current evaluator
still uses exact linear-path matching. Therefore, Top-1 Exact Path and Oracle
Exact Path scores of $0\%$ for these two groups may reflect a limitation of
the metric rather than a complete system failure.

\paragraph{Answer and citation metrics are only proxies.}

Token F1 and ROUGE-L measure surface overlap and do not guarantee a legally
correct answer. The citation metric compares sets of statutory article
identifiers but does not verify semantic entailment between each assertion
and the cited article. Normalized Exact Match is also unsuitable for answers
that include additional explanations and evidence markers. Automated metrics
therefore cannot replace expert evaluation.

\subsection{Internal validity}

\paragraph{Circularity between the graph and the benchmark.}

The questions, gold rules, gold events, and many negative examples were
constructed from the same graph used by the system for retrieval. This design
is useful for testing the ability to recover a known encoding, but it may
increase compatibility between the benchmark and the pipeline's
representation. The results do not demonstrate that the system can handle an
independently authored legal question or an out-of-distribution formulation.

\paragraph{The paired ablation is controlled but not sufficiently
discriminative.}

The two configurations use the same benchmark, retrieval resources,
thresholds, answer generator, and evaluator; the changed variable is
\texttt{counterfactual\_mode}. This reduces confounding in the comparison.
However, the benchmark lacks cases involving an alternative mechanism,
exception, conflict, or unknown root for which hard intervention and node
deletion yield different results. The null quality result may therefore be
due to the benchmark's low sensitivity and does not establish that the two
methods are equivalent.

\paragraph{Query analysis and event matching remain heuristic.}

The identification of claim type, answer intent, and mediator relies in part
on Vietnamese regular expressions, keywords, and semantic mappings. These
heuristics may fail on complex negation, novel formulations, homonymous
terms, or queries containing multiple claims. Errors at this stage may affect
both the verification decision and the form of the answer.

\paragraph{Runtime has not been reproduced on disclosed hardware.}

The two runs were executed on the same server, but the operating system,
Python version, CPU, RAM, and GPU have not yet been entered in
Table~\ref{tab:environment}. The structural SCM's approximately $3.23$ times
higher mean runtime per sample and $2.85$ times higher wall-clock runtime are
valid only for the environment in which the runs were conducted. No repeated
experiments have been performed with cold caches, warm caches, or multiple
hardware configurations.

\subsection{Data and model limitations}

\paragraph{Rule normalization may lose legal nuance.}

The corpus transforms statutory text into conditions, effects, subjects, and
articles. This process may simplify modal expressions such as ``may,''
conjunctive conditions, exceptions, cross-references, and scopes of
application. Event canonicalization may also merge two near-synonymous
expressions that differ legally, or retain multiple aliases within the same
display name.

\paragraph{LegalSCM is deterministic and limited.}

The model uses three states
$\{\mathrm{TRUE},\mathrm{FALSE},\mathrm{UNKNOWN}\}$ and fixed-point
inference over the encoded rules. The current version does not fully
represent:

\begin{itemize}
    \item multiple simultaneous conditions and nested conditional structures;
    \item negation, exceptions, and defeasible rules;
    \item document hierarchy, rule priority, and interpretive conflicts;
    \item temporal validity and changes in the law;
    \item probability, confidence levels, or epistemic uncertainty;
    \item the abduction step from observed facts to a factual context.
\end{itemize}

LegalSCM is therefore not a complete model of legal reasoning or real-world
causality.

\paragraph{Search is constrained by a budget.}

The retriever expands at most two hops, whereas path ablation searches for
alternative paths of at most three hops and limits the number of candidates.
The statement ``no alternative path exists'' in the artifacts means only that
no path was found within the graph and the configured search budget; it does
not prove that no other legal mechanism exists.

\subsection{External validity}

The corpus focuses only on the Vietnamese Penal Code of 2015 and the amended
content included in the data. The study has not evaluated:

\begin{itemize}
    \item legal documents in civil, administrative, or commercial law;
    \item case law, guiding resolutions, case files, or adjudicative practice;
    \item new documents or formulations absent from the corpus;
    \item questions authored by real users or independent experts;
    \item languages other than Vietnamese;
    \item graphs of substantially greater scale and depth.
\end{itemize}

Because the synthetic benchmark was generated from the same source graph,
the results cannot be directly generalized to open-domain legal question
answering or real-world legal advisory systems.

\subsection{Conclusion validity}

The benchmark contains $250$ samples, with some very small groups, such as
only four branch samples and six bridge-convergence fallback samples.
Group-level rates for these categories have high variance and should not be
regarded as stable estimates.

The study includes only one complete run per mode for the primary artifacts
and does not report confidence intervals or sample-level statistical tests.
Because the two configurations produce identical quality metrics, there is
no basis for claiming that structural SCM improves accuracy. Similarly,
Oracle Exact Path is only an upper-bound analysis over the candidate set and
does not represent the deployable performance of the Top-1 selector.

The difficulty labels have also not been calibrated independently of question
type. The higher Answer F1 for the hard group than for the medium group
reflects sample composition and does not demonstrate that the system handles
hard questions better.

\subsection{Expert validation and legal currency}

The rules, event normalization, questions, gold answers, gold paths, and
citations have not been fully validated by an independent panel of legal
experts. In addition, the corpus must be versioned and annotated with
effective dates to prevent the use of provisions that have been amended or
are no longer in force. Until both requirements are satisfied, the results
should be regarded only as a technical evaluation on a research benchmark.

\subsection{Responsible use}

The system is a research prototype, not a legal advisory tool, and must not
be used to make automated decisions that affect an individual's rights or
obligations. An answer with citations may still rely on an incorrect path,
missing statutory provisions, or an incomplete rule representation.

If developed into a support application, the system should:

\begin{itemize}
    \item clearly display the \texttt{UNCERTAIN} state rather than forcing it
    into a conclusion;
    \item allow inspection of every rule, article, and hop in the path;
    \item record the corpus version, model, code, and query time;
    \item warn that the output must be verified by an expert;
    \item protect personal data if real case files or scenarios are processed
    in the future;
    \item maintain mechanisms for updating and withdrawing outdated legal
    knowledge.
\end{itemize}

These limitations define the boundaries of every claim made in this study
and provide the basis for the next experimental stage.
